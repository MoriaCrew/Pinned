# Start flow samples

Three synthetic sticker cut-outs shown on the mini boards of the
look picker in `/new` (PIN-101): a rubber duck, red sneakers and a
straw hat. They are shown in product UI, so they live here rather
than in `public/lab/` (which is for the `/lab/ui` page only).

Copied unchanged from `public/lab/cutout-*.webp`, which come from the
project's own eval fixtures (`eval/fixtures/studio/elements/*-flat.jpg`,
generated with Higgsfield text-to-image, white background removed by
SAM 3.1 in the PIN-50 spike, resized to WebP). No real people and no
real photos (see `eval/README.md`).
